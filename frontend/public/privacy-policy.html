Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This policy explains how Dmytro Barchuk (“we”) handles information when you use clubworms, a service for creating and participating in book, movie, sport, and board game clubs. For privacy questions or requests, contact dvbarchuk@gmail.com.

Information we process

  • Account and sign-in information. You can sign in with Google or with an email and password through Supabase Auth. When you choose Google sign-in, the app requests the basic OpenID Connect scopes openid, email, and profile. We receive and use the Supabase account identifier associated with your Google sign-in, your email address, display name, and profile picture when available to authenticate you and create or maintain your clubworms profile. We do not request access to Google Drive, Gmail, contacts, or other Google services.
  • Profile information. Your display name and, if you choose to provide them, avatar URL, date of birth, and bio.
  • Club and event information. Clubs you create or join, membership and role information, invite and deletion requests, event titles, dates and statuses, and content you submit to the service.
  • Preferences and session information. The app stores your language and theme preferences in your browser. Supabase Auth stores the authentication session needed to keep you signed in.
  • Movie search queries. When you search for a movie, the search text is sent to The Movie Database (TMDB) to return movie results. Google account information is not sent to TMDB.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, create and maintain profiles, operate clubs and events, display club content to the relevant users, respond to support requests, protect the service, and meet legal obligations. Google user data is used only to provide sign-in and account profile features described in this policy. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to develop or improve generalized AI or machine-learning models.

When information is shared

We use service providers to operate the app: Supabase provides authentication and database services; Google provides Google sign-in and cloud hosting; and TMDB provides movie search results. These providers process information as needed to provide their services. We do not sell personal information. Your display name, profile image, club membership, and club content may be visible to other members of your clubs; content in public clubs may be visible to users who can access those clubs. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect users and the service.

Storage, security, and retention

Account and application data is stored using Supabase and the app’s hosting infrastructure. We use access controls and HTTPS in the deployed service, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. We keep information while your account is active and as needed to operate the service. You may request access, correction, or deletion by contacting us. We will assess and respond to requests under applicable law; some information may need to be retained for legal, security, or operational reasons.

Your choices and rights

You can edit your display name, avatar, date of birth, and bio in your profile. You can change your browser language and theme preferences at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing of your personal information. Contact us to make a request or ask a question.

Children and changes

clubworms is a general club service and is not designed to collect personal information from children who are not permitted to use online services under the laws that apply to them. We may update this policy as the service or applicable requirements change. The effective date above indicates the latest revision.

Ми отримуємо та використовуємо ідентифікатор облікового запису Supabase, пов’язаного з вашим входом через Google, адресу електронної пошти, ім’я та фото профілю, якщо вони доступні, щоб автентифікувати вас і створити або підтримувати профіль clubworms.
